## Quick Facts About Your Privacy

✅ **No username/password accounts** — you can use everything except cloud backup without signing in to anything  
✅ **Your content stays with you** — calculations, notes, and settings are stored locally on your device; we never see them  
✅ **Optional cloud backup** — backups go to a hidden folder in **your own Google Drive**, not to our servers  
✅ **Optional Google sign-in** — used only for backup; it creates a minimal sign-in record (email, name, photo) that you can have deleted at any time  
✅ **You're in control** — delete your backup and sign out directly in the app ("Delete Data & Sign Out"), and see the [deletion page](account-deletion) for everything else

## About MinteCalc

MinteCalc is a feature-rich calculator app available on iOS and Android, offering:

- **Currency Converter** — Convert between world currencies with regularly updated rates
- **Unit Converter** — Convert measurements across various unit types
